# Cubist Illustration

A photo broken into flat, overlapping geometric planes seen from several viewpoints at once, in bright colors with bold dark outlines.

## Prompt

```text
Convert the attached photo into an original Cubist illustration. Avoid copying any specific existing artwork.

Multiple viewpoints: push the distortion far. It must not look like a naturalistic drawing or stained glass cut into facets. Break the subject into fragments and show several viewpoints at the same time instead of a single perspective. For people, clearly split each face into a profile and a front view at once, with two large almond-shaped eyes at different angles and heights, a sharply angled nose, and a displaced mouth. Use a different color for each half of the face. Reshape bodies into bold, simplified, disjointed geometric masses rather than natural anatomy.

Planes: build everything from a few large, flat, overlapping geometric planes such as triangles, quadrilaterals, arcs, and circles, parallel to the picture surface. Fill a few planes with simple stripes, dots, or grid patterns. Keep the space shallow, with little depth or shading and no realistic perspective.

Subject: keep the people, animals, and objects from the photo, along with their main colors, clothing, hair, accessories, and overall arrangement, so the subject stays recognizable despite the fragmentation. Don't add major objects that aren't in the photo.

Background: break the photo's setting into the same geometric planes, so subject and background interlock.

Color: bright, varied blocks of color based on the photo's colors, with strong contrast between neighboring planes.

Line: thick black outlines around the main shapes, slightly irregular and hand-drawn.

Surface: solid, flat color with no mottled or watercolor texture. No gradients, photographic texture, or 3D rendering.

Don't include any text, logos, or watermarks.

Keep the same aspect ratio as the attached image.
```

## Observed in references

These traits come from viewing Google image results for Cubism illustrations. The images weren't stored. Add the ones you want to the image prompt. This style follows the colorful illustration approach those results favor.

- **Eyes:** eyes are often the main focal point, drawn with concentric rings.
- **Palette:** many combine orange, teal, and yellow with black, or use a single vivid background color.
- **Painted versions:** some look like muted brown and gray paintings, closer to early Cubism.
- **Low-poly images:** gem-like faceted hearts and animals also appear in the results, but they use a different look that this style doesn't follow.

## Examples

- [bicycle-repair.webp](examples/bicycle-repair.webp): conversion of a generated photo of two people repairing a bicycle on a street, made with `gpt-image` using the split-face, disjointed body, thick outline, flat plane, and pattern rules now in the prompt, given as additions at the time. Without those rules, an earlier attempt kept natural figures and looked closer to stained glass. This result has no text, keeps the 3:2 ratio, and shows two-color split faces, thick outlines, and striped, dotted, and grid planes around a recognizable bicycle. Faint scratch-like marks and slight mottling remain, and the bodies are still fairly natural under their faceted shapes. Some details are lost: the toolbox is cut off, the rag is gone, and the man's curls are simplified.
