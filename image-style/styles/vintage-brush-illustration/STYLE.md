# Vintage Brush Illustration

A photo reinterpreted as a flat hand-brushed illustration in only a few muted colors on cream paper, with thin offset outlines and no text.

## Prompt

```text
Convert the attached photo into a hand-drawn brush illustration.
The subject in the photo may be food, a product, a landscape, an animal, a person, or an object. Whatever it is, make it the main character as it is.

[Style]
- A digital gouache/brush pen painting feel. A hand drawing where brush marks, rough edges, and slightly spilled coloring stay visible
- Don't depict the photo realistically; reinterpret it as a flat illustration with simply organized forms
- No gradients, shadows, dimensionality, or texture rendering at all. Completely flat
- Outlines are thin, irregular brush lines, slightly offset from the colored areas

[Color]
- The background is a cream/off-white paper color
- Use only 3-4 colors extracted from the attached photo (1 deep main color + 1 strong accent color + 1-2 supporting colors)
- A vintage tone with slightly lowered saturation. Never increase the number of colors

[Composition]
- Place the subject large at the center of the frame, at 50-55% of the canvas
- Leave generous margins on all sides

[Forbidden]
- Realistic rendering, 3D, shadows, reflections, logos, watermarks, border frames, and text of any kind
- Multiplying the subject into several

[Ratio]
Same as the attached image.
```

## Observed in references

The user's original reference images aren't stored. They showed these traits, which the prompt doesn't state. Add the ones you want to the image prompt. The references also have large brush lettering and small handwritten phrases, which this style removes by the user's choice.

- **Detail:** complex subjects, such as a tower or a food platter, keep many small brushed details even with few colors.
- **Scene:** most subjects sit on bare cream, with little or no setting. Any scenery is a loose patch of brush strokes, such as a sky or grass, that stops well before the edges.
- **Brush grain:** dry-brush streaks and print-like speckles break up the color fills, letting the paper show through.
- **Palette:** the limit is applied loosely. Some images use more than four colors, and a person's or food's natural colors stay.
- **Outlines:** some subjects have thin dark outlines, while others use none and rely on color shapes.

## Examples

- [bicycle.webp](examples/bicycle.webp): conversion of a generated photo of a red bicycle against a wall, made with `gpt-image` from the prompt above plus rougher brushwork and a wider margin request. Without those additions, an earlier attempt had cleaner grain and spanned about 80% of the width. This result has no text, keeps the 3:2 ratio, and shows the flat, muted, few-color look with clear dry-brush edges. It drops the wall, flowers, and shadows and keeps only small weeds from the setting, and it still spans about 70% of the width.
