# Gouache Marker Illustration

A photo reinterpreted as a cute, simplified flat illustration with thick wobbly outlines and visible gouache and marker texture on cream, with no border or text.

## Prompt

```text
[Style]
Reinterpret the attached photo as a flat illustration with a hand-drawn gouache/marker texture rather than realistic rendering. Make outlines thick and slightly wobbly, with visible grain where the brush or marker passed. Express it in cute, simplified forms.

[Central illustration]
Simplify the subject into a hand-drawn illustration, referencing the original photo's composition and colors.

[Bottom props]
Add 1-2 illustrated props that suit the subject in the bottom left and right corners (for example, raw ingredients, a drinking glass, or a napkin/fabric pattern). They may be added even if they aren't in the original photo, as long as they fit naturally.

[Background]
Fixed ivory/cream flat background.

[Color]
Without a fixed palette, analyze the colors and mood of the subject in the attached photo and automatically choose 2-3 suitable accent colors, applied consistently to the props. Keep the subject's own colors.

[Border and text]
No border or frame. No text anywhere in the image, including titles, captions, labels, ingredient lists, or handwritten notes.

[Ratio]
Same as the attached image.
```

## Observed in references

The user's original reference images aren't stored. They showed these traits, which the prompt doesn't state. Add the ones you want to the image prompt. The references also have a wavy border, a title, and a caption, which this style removes by the user's choice.

- **Scene:** subjects sit in their setting, such as a park, a city skyline, or a snowy field, when the source has one. Single animals and objects often sit on bare cream instead.
- **Sky:** skies are rough diagonal strokes in pale blue that stop unevenly before the paper's edge, with scribbled white clouds.
- **Outlines:** black outlines are strongest on foreground subjects and props. Distant scenery is often painted without them.
- **Texture:** color areas look like dry-brush gouache or crayon with visible paper grain, not smooth fills.
- **Detail:** complex subjects, such as a tower or a food platter, keep many small details, while plush toys and animals stay simple.
- **Props:** props are small, self-contained sticker-like drawings, sometimes with a soft cream shadow.

## Examples

- [bicycle.webp](examples/bicycle.webp): conversion of a generated photo of a red bicycle against a wall, made with `gpt-image` from the prompt above plus black outlines, dry-brush texture, a loosely painted setting, and a limit of 2 props. Without those additions, an earlier attempt had speckled colored outlines, no wall or ground, and three props. This result has no border or text, keeps the source's 3:2 ratio, and matches the references' solid outlines and rough strokes. The tree shadows on the wall became green leaf-like strokes, and the mug and napkin were added by the model.
