# Egon Schiele

A photo redrawn as an Egon Schiele-style Expressionist gouache and watercolor drawing, with nervous angular contour lines, gaunt elongated figures, bony hands, and patchy, sickly skin colors on bare buff paper.

## Prompt

```text
Convert the attached photo into an original work on paper in the style of Egon Schiele (Austrian Expressionism, 1910–1918), made with pencil or black chalk, gouache, and watercolor. Don't copy any specific existing Schiele work.

Line: draw every figure with a confident, nervous, angular contour line in black or dark brown that changes thickness and breaks into sharp corners and jagged turns. Keep the line visible everywhere, as the main structure of the image.

Figures: make people thin, elongated, and angular, with sharp elbows, knees, and shoulders, and slightly twisted, tense poses. Give them large, bony hands with long, spread, angular fingers. Faces are gaunt and intense, with deep-set, staring eyes, sharp cheekbones, and messy hair, while keeping the photo's expressions.

Skin: color skin with patchy, uneven washes of ochre, orange, and pale yellow mixed with blotches of red, pink, green, and blue, especially at joints, knuckles, cheeks, and around the eyes, so the skin looks raw and slightly sickly.

Clothing: fill clothing with flat, loose gouache areas in earthy or muted colors, such as dark browns, black, olive, deep red, and blue, with visible brush marks and some angular fold lines. Leave parts unpainted.

Halo: surround each figure with a thin, rough band of opaque white gouache along its outline, like a glowing halo against the paper.

Background: leave most of the background as bare warm buff or beige paper. Suggest the setting from the photo only with a few sparse lines and light washes, and keep it much less finished than the figures.

Subject: keep the photo's people, poses, expressions, clothing colors, objects, and composition recognizable. Draw objects with the same angular line and simple washes. Don't add people, objects, or costumes that aren't in the photo.

Surface: gouache, watercolor, and pencil on toned paper, with paper texture, uneven washes, and dry brush marks. No oil paint, smooth digital shading, or photographic detail.

Show only the work filling the frame, with no frame, mat, wall, or border.

Don't include any text, signature, monogram, or watermark.

Keep the same aspect ratio as the attached image.
```

## Observed in references

These traits come from viewing Google image results for "egon schiele portrait watercolor gouache" and "egon schiele town houses painting", and from the sources below. The images weren't stored. Add the ones you want to the image prompt.

- **Portraits:** most works are single figures or self-portraits, often seen from slightly above, with hands raised near the face and spread fingers.
- **Patterns:** some clothing has bold stripes or blocks of color, such as a striped skirt or a patterned blouse.
- **Signature:** many works carry a boxed signature and date, which this style leaves out.
- **Towns:** Schiele also painted dense, flattened oil views of old town houses in muted browns, ochres, and greens, with patchwork windows and laundry lines. This style follows his figure works on paper instead.
- **Nudes:** many famous works are nudes, which this style doesn't follow. It keeps the photo's clothing.
- **Overlap:** `edvard-munch` uses thin oil paint with flowing wave lines and dark moody skies, while this style uses angular contour lines, patchy watercolor skin, and bare paper.
