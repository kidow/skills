# Rembrandt

A photo repainted as a Rembrandt-style Dutch Golden Age oil painting, with dramatic chiaroscuro, warm golden light on faces and hands emerging from deep brown darkness, a muted earthy palette, and thick impasto highlights.

## Prompt

```text
Convert the attached photo into an original oil painting in the style of Rembrandt van Rijn (Dutch Golden Age, Baroque, 1630s–1660s). Don't copy any specific existing Rembrandt painting.

Light: use strong chiaroscuro. A single warm, golden light falls from one side and slightly above, picking out the faces, hands, and the most important objects, while the rest of the scene sinks gradually into deep, warm shadow. On faces, let the light model the forms softly, with a small triangle of light on the shadowed cheek where it fits.

Darkness: let large parts of the background and edges dissolve into deep brown, olive, and near-black darkness with soft, warm transitions and no hard cutoff. Keep the photo's setting only as dim, simplified shapes barely visible in the shadows.

Color: a limited, muted earthy palette of raw and burnt umber, sienna, yellow ochre, warm gold, lead white, and deep reddish browns, with the photo's clothing colors kept but darkened and warmed. Avoid bright, saturated, or cool modern colors.

Paint: thick, textured impasto with bold, dashing strokes in the brightest highlights, such as on faces, hands, and metal, and thin, smooth, transparent glazes in the shadows. Let some brushwork stay loose and rough, with a few scratched or dragged marks.

Figures: paint people with deep psychological realism, carefully observed faces and hands, and natural expressions from the photo, quiet and introspective in mood.

Subject: keep the photo's people, poses, expressions, clothing, objects, and composition recognizable. Don't add people, objects, or historical costumes that aren't in the photo.

Surface: aged oil painting on canvas, with warm, slightly yellowed varnish and fine craquelure. No photographic detail, digital gradients, or 3D rendering.

Show only the painting filling the frame, with no frame, wall, or border.

Don't include any text, signature, or watermark.

Keep the same aspect ratio as the attached image.
```

## Observed in references

These traits come from viewing Google image results for "rembrandt paintings" and from the sources below. The images weren't stored. Add the ones you want to the image prompt.

- **Portraits:** many works are half-length portraits and self-portraits with berets, wide hats, lace collars, and turbans against plain dark backgrounds.
- **Groups:** group scenes, such as militia companies and anatomy lessons, light a few key faces and leave the rest in shadow.
- **Bible scenes:** biblical and historical scenes use glowing light sources and rich gold, red, and white fabrics.
- **Seascapes:** rare dramatic seascapes show stormy skies with a single beam of light.
- **Prints:** Rembrandt also made many etchings, which this style doesn't follow. See `etching-engraving` for line prints.
- **Overlap:** `graphic-novel` also uses dramatic light, but with ink lines and flat colors, while this style uses painterly oil, warm darkness, and impasto.
