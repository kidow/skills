# Alphonse Mucha

A photo redrawn as an Alphonse Mucha-style Art Nouveau decorative panel, with graceful outlined figures, flowing whiplash curves, a circular halo motif, flowers, and soft pastel lithograph colors.

## Prompt

```text
Convert the attached photo into an original decorative illustration in the style of Alphonse Mucha's Art Nouveau posters and decorative panels (Paris, 1895–1905), printed as a color lithograph. Don't copy any specific existing Mucha work.

Line: draw every form with clean, elegant, hand-drawn contour lines in dark brown or dark olive, thicker on outer silhouettes and finer inside, with smooth, flowing curves. Turn hair, fabric, scarves, smoke, and plant stems into long, rhythmic whiplash curves and arabesques.

Color: flat areas of soft, muted pastel color with only gentle shading, in a harmonious palette of sage and olive green, dusty rose, peach, warm ochre, soft gold, pale blue, lavender, and cream, based on the photo's colors.

Figures: idealize people gracefully, with serene, softly modeled faces, rosy cheeks, and delicate features, while keeping the photo's expressions. Let their hair flow in curling strands, and add flowers or leaves woven into hair or clothing only where it fits naturally.

Halo: place a large circular or arched halo motif behind the main figure's head or upper body, filled with fine concentric bands, small geometric or mosaic patterns, and stylized flowers.

Background: simplify the photo's setting into flat, decorative planes, with stylized flowers, leaves, and vines growing along the curves. Keep some calm, plain areas so the figures stand out.

Subject: keep the photo's people, poses, expressions, clothing colors, objects, and composition recognizable. Draw objects with the same flowing outlines and pastel colors. Don't add people or objects that aren't in the photo.

Surface: color lithograph on cream paper, with slightly soft, matte ink, faint print grain, and a gently aged look. No photographic detail, digital gradients, or 3D rendering.

Show only the illustration filling the frame, with no ornamental border, frame, mat, title panel, or wall.

Don't include any text, lettering, signature, or watermark.

Keep the same aspect ratio as the attached image.
```

## Observed in references

These traits come from viewing Google image results for "alphonse mucha art" and from the sources below. The images weren't stored. Add the ones you want to the image prompt.

- **Women:** almost every work centers on one young woman with long, curling hair, often wearing flowing robes and flower crowns.
- **Formats:** most works are tall, narrow panels, which this style doesn't follow, since it keeps the source's ratio.
- **Borders:** many panels have ornate frames, mosaic borders, and title lettering at the top or bottom, which this style leaves out.
- **Series:** decorative series show seasons, flowers, arts, and times of day as allegorical women surrounded by matching plants.
- **Late works:** the Slav Epic paintings are huge, realistic, misty canvases in blue tones, which this style doesn't follow.
- **Overlap:** `gustav-klimt` uses gold leaf and dense mosaic patterns, while this style uses clean outlines, flowing curves, and soft pastel lithograph color.
