# Claude Monet

In the style of French Impressionist painter Claude Monet: a photo repainted in broken brushstrokes of vibrating color that capture a moment of natural light.

## Prompt

```text
Convert the attached photo into an original oil painting in the Impressionist style of Claude Monet. Avoid copying any specific existing artwork.

Brushwork: build the entire image from short, visible, broken brushstrokes of oil paint on canvas. Paint thinly and lightly, with dry, feathery, quick strokes of varied size and direction, not thick, even impasto or a uniform pattern of dabs. Let the canvas show through in places. Vary the strokes to suit each surface: quick dabs for foliage and flowers, horizontal touches for water and reflections, and longer flowing strokes for fabric and sky.

Form: paint as if seen at a glance in the light, not studied in detail. The subject is recognizable as soft masses of color, with edges dissolving into the surrounding strokes. Render thin structures as loose, broken trails of strokes, or omit them. No hard outlines or thin, sharp, continuous lines.

Light: the painting is about a fleeting moment of natural outdoor light and atmosphere. Keep the photo's time of day and light direction, and let light and air soften the whole scene.

Color: place pure, luminous colors side by side rather than blending them smoothly, using complementary contrasts such as orange against blue and pink against green. Avoid black. Paint shadows in blue, violet, and green rather than gray or black. Keep the colors of the subject recognizable from the photo, but enrich them with touches of neighboring hues.

Subject: keep the photo's subject, setting, composition, and main colors. Don't add major objects that aren't in the photo. People remain loosely painted, with little facial detail.

Surface: visible canvas weave with a painterly finish. No photographic detail, digital smoothness, or line drawing.

Don't include any text, signature, or watermark.

Keep the same aspect ratio as the attached image.
```

## Observed in references

These traits come from viewing Google image results for Monet's style. The images weren't stored. Add the ones you want to the image prompt.

- **Water:** reflections of sky and trees cover much of the surface, with no clear horizon, and scattered water lilies.
- **Sky:** blue skies are full of soft white and pink clouds with visible strokes.
- **Haze:** morning scenes dissolve into a single blue-green or orange-gray tone, with one bright accent such as a small orange sun.
- **Late works:** the final garden paintings are darker and denser, in reds and browns, with forms almost dissolved.
- **Exceptions:** a few portraits and still lifes have clearer drawing and stronger, flatter color.
