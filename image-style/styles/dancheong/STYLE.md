# Dancheong

A photo repainted in the style of Dancheong, the traditional decorative painting on Korean wooden architecture, with five cardinal colors, banded color steps, white and black lines, and cloud and floral patterns.

## Prompt

```text
Convert the attached photo into an original illustration in the style of Dancheong, the traditional decorative painting on Korean palaces and temples.

Color: use the five cardinal colors (obangsaek) of blue, red, yellow, white, and black, together with the traditional Dancheong base colors of muted blue-green and deep red-brown. Colors are vivid but mineral and slightly matte, like pigment on wood.

Banded color steps: render forms, especially the subject, with flat bands of color that step from light to dark, such as pale green to green to deep green, or white to pink to red, each band separated by a thin line. Don't use smooth gradients or realistic shading.

Line: outline every shape and band with crisp black ink lines, and add thin white separation lines along edges, as in Dancheong.

Patterns: decorate the subject's larger surfaces with Dancheong motifs, such as lotus and peony panels, and add only a few accents to the setting, such as some cloud scrolls or a single band of brocade or lotus pattern. Don't cover the subject or the whole background with patterns, and keep open parts, such as wheels or windows, simple.

Subject: keep the photo's subject as the clear focus, and keep its setting, composition, and main colors recognizable, redrawn as flat, stylized decorative forms. Keep the background mostly as calm flat color areas that follow the photo's colors, so a pale wall stays pale. Don't add major objects or animals that aren't in the photo.

Surface: flat painted decoration with crisp edges, like fresh Dancheong on wood. No photographic detail, 3D rendering, or brushy painting texture.

Don't include any text, frame, border, or watermark.

Keep the same aspect ratio as the attached image.
```

## Observed in references

These traits come from the example image on the user's linked prompt page, whose prompt was only a short general description. The image wasn't stored. Add the ones you want to the image prompt.

- **Background:** a deep navy background makes the colors stand out.
- **Motifs:** a red-crowned crane, a pine tree, a red sun, layered green mountains, and peonies appear together, like traditional longevity symbols.
- **Mountains:** mountains are layered green ridges outlined with thin gold and white lines.
- **Frame:** the image sits in a circular medallion with an ornamental border of flowers and cloud patterns, which this style leaves out.
