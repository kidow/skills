# Bold Organic Shapes

A food photo abstracted into oversized, hand-cut organic color masses in 2-4 bold colors, with asymmetrical cropping and a matte print finish, with no text.

## Prompt

```text
# Modern abstract graphic system - extremely important
Based on the uploaded food image, create an ultra-minimal abstract graphic editorial artwork.

The core mood is:
- Matisse-inspired cutout graphic
- Scandinavian poster design
- Modern art gallery poster
- Geometric food abstraction
- Pinterest save-core
- Flat vector graphic energy
- Neo-minimal composition
- Bold primary color blocking
- Designer lifestyle branding

Important: the core of this image is not a realistic food photo. It is a highly sensory design artwork that reinterprets food as a graphic object.

Never use: the food photo as it is, a general food advertising feel, delivery app design, a realistic restaurant poster, a default AI template, complex detail, or a photorealistic style.

# Abstract food transformation system - critical
Analyze the uploaded food image and always keep:
- The core silhouette
- The food's formal characteristics
- The representative colors
- The food's identity

While doing so, reinterpret the food as a simplified organic shape, a flat graphic object, an abstract cutout form, an oversized color mass, and a bold minimal illustration.

Important: the food should feel like vector art, paper cutout, a silkscreen poster, matte graphic texture, and a hand-cut abstract form.

Never use: highly detailed illustration, 3D rendering, realistic shading, or photoreal food.

# Color system - extremely important
Color must feel based on strong primary colors, with a limited palette, high-contrast minimalism, bold graphic balance, and a museum poster palette.

Possible color combinations:
- Warm gray + red + black
- Muted pastel background + bold hero object
- Ivory + scarlet + dark green
- Butter yellow + forest green + charcoal black
- Dusty pink + espresso brown + warm beige
- Sage green + burgundy + soft ivory
- Muted lavender + olive green + paper white
- Dark chocolate + pale mint + warm gray
- Smoky teal + burnt orange + ivory
- Deep wine + blush beige + soft black

Important: don't use many colors. Use only 2-4 main colors.

Never use: blue, unless the subject itself is blue; a rainbow palette; excessive gradients; neon cyberpunk; or muddy colors.

# Composition system - critical
The structure must feel like giant negative space, intentional imbalance, floating abstract objects, modular visual rhythm, asymmetrical hierarchy, clean breathing zones, and a gallery poster composition.

Automatically judge which object to enlarge greatly, where to boldly leave empty, which elements to crop, and where to place tension.

Important: an overly safe layout is absolutely forbidden.

# Graphic object arrangement - very important
Use only objects derived from elements in the uploaded image. Don't add major objects that aren't in it.
Food-based objects:
- Must not be scattered randomly; they need intentional rhythm
- Have strong size contrast
- Some are extremely oversized
- Some are tiny accent objects
- Some overflow beyond the frame

Possible arrangements: repetition, floating arrangement, stacked composition, and cropped edge interaction.

# Texture system - important
The overall texture should feel like matte print texture, premium paper grain, subtle ink bleed, soft silkscreen imperfection, and a slightly tactile poster finish.

Possible: fine paper texture, very faint grain, a feeling of ink bleed, and printed poster imperfection.

Important: an overly clean AI vector feel is forbidden. It needs some of the feel of real printed matter.

# Text
Don't include any text, letters, numbers, or typography.

# Final visual goal - extremely important
The final result must:
- Look like the work of a real, famous design studio
- Strongly invite saving on Pinterest
- Look like it could appear in an art book
- Look like a modern gallery poster
- Be good enough to sell as design merchandise
- Make people feel "wow, this has real taste"

Important: the result should look like modern graphic art rather than a food poster.

# Ratio
Keep the same aspect ratio as the uploaded image.
```

## Observed in references

The user's original reference images aren't stored. They showed these traits, which the prompt doesn't state. Add the ones you want to the image prompt. The references also have oversized cropped typography, small editorial text, and large cobalt blue shapes, which this style removes by the user's choice.

- **Palette:** most use a cream or warm beige ground with tomato red, sometimes adding yellow or black.
- **Abstraction:** subjects reduce to very few shapes, such as a croissant as three red pieces with small cream flecks.
- **Paper gaps:** thin slivers of the background separate parts of a subject.
- **Scale:** subjects are large and sometimes run off the frame's edge.

## Examples

- [waffle.webp](examples/waffle.webp): conversion of a generated photo of a waffle with strawberries and butter on a plate, made with `gpt-image` from the prompt above plus requests for no blue, only source colors, a cropped asymmetrical waffle, and simple strawberry centers. Without those additions, an earlier attempt added large blue shapes and about six colors, kept the waffle mostly in frame, and had hand-like strawberry centers. This result has no text or blue, keeps the source's 3:2 ratio, and shows rounded masses, a strong crop, wide empty cream, and print grain. Besides cream, orange, red, and green, it also has pale butter and pink tones, so it's slightly over the 2-4 color limit.
