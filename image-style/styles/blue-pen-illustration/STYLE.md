# Blue Pen Illustration

A photo redrawn as a decorative contemporary pen-and-ink illustration, with supple dark-blue pen contours, rhythmic architectural linework, restrained flat colors, stylized botanical shapes, and generous pure-white sky and paper.

## Prompt

```text
Convert the attached photo into a decorative contemporary pen-and-ink illustration on pure-white paper.

Line: supple dark-blue pen contours for everything, including buildings, people, plants, and objects, with rhythmic architectural linework and selective decorative patterning, such as roof tiles, window grids, brick courses, and paving stones. No black outlines.

Color: restrained flat colors inspired by the place in the photo, filled inside the pen lines without gradients, soft shading, or texture.

Plants: turn trees, shrubs, and flowers into stylized botanical shapes, with leaves as simple clustered forms and flowers as small flat blossoms.

Perspective: gently flatten the perspective while keeping buildings and objects believable, preserving their authentic silhouettes, proportions, and ornament.

Sky and paper: leave the sky and other empty areas as generous pure-white paper. Don't color or texture the sky.

Subject: keep the photo's people, poses, clothing, objects, setting, and composition recognizable. Don't add people, objects, or buildings that aren't in the photo.

Keep inscriptions on signs and buildings blank. No lettering, logos, borders, signatures, or watermarks.

Keep the same aspect ratio as the attached image.
```

## Observed in references

These traits come from five city posters the user shared (Busan, Seoul, Daegu, Daejeon, and Incheon), made with a prompt for new city posters. The images weren't stored. Add the ones you want to the image prompt.

- **Palette:** the same palette repeats across the posters: teal and pale blue glass, cream and beige stone, brick red, sage and leaf greens, coral and pink flowers, and navy for every line.
- **Linework:** lines are even and fine, a little thicker on outlines than on details. Windows, tiles, and bricks are drawn one by one, while walls and roads stay mostly flat.
- **Plants:** leaves are drawn as small rounded clusters in two greens, and flower beds are clusters of coral, pink, cream, or yellow blossoms.
- **Framing:** each poster has a leafy branch entering from the top right corner and flower beds in the lower corners, framing the scene.
- **People:** a few small, simply drawn pedestrians walk on a pale paved path, with no detailed faces.
- **Sky:** the upper third or more is plain white with no clouds.
- **Title:** each poster has the city name in small navy uppercase sans-serif at the upper left, which this style leaves out.
- **Layout:** the posters are 4:5 vertical and combine a main landmark with two supporting landmarks along a curved path. This style keeps the photo's own aspect ratio and scene instead.
- **Overlap:** `colored-pencil-sketch` uses loose pencil strokes, `gouache-marker-illustration` uses thick wobbly outlines on cream, and `etching-engraving` uses black hatching, while this style uses clean dark-blue pen lines, flat colors, and white paper.
