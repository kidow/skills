# Midcentury Cut Paper

A food photo reinterpreted as a bold geometric cut-paper illustration in a few flat midcentury colors on one solid background, with no text.

## Prompt

```text
# Core objective
Automatically analyze the attached food photo and reinterpret it as an illustration, not a simple food photo, menu image, delivery app thumbnail, or realistic advertising image, with the feel of:
- Mid-Century Modern Poster
- Bauhaus Inspired Graphic Design
- Scandinavian Modern Art
- Matisse Cut-Paper Style
- Museum Gift Shop Print
- Vintage Modernist Illustration
- Designer Wall Art

Combine a geometric food illustration with strong color blocks.

Keep the same aspect ratio as the attached image.

# Automatic food analysis
Analyze the main food, silhouette, form, characteristic structure, ingredients, colors, texture, and mood of the uploaded image, and automatically determine what the food is.

# Food reinterpretation
Don't copy the original photo as it is. Instead, simplify it geometrically in a Mid-Century Modern style.
- Round shapes become simple circles
- Leaves become simple organic curves
- Cheese becomes bold triangles
- Pizza becomes a thick slice form
- A burger becomes a combination of semicircles and rectangles
- Fruit becomes flat color blocks

However, the type of food must be instantly recognizable.

# Composition
Place the food at the center as the single main subject.

# Color system
Analyze the food's colors and generate a Mid-Century Modern palette, for example cream, butter yellow, tomato red, burnt orange, teal, turquoise, forest green, sage green, deep purple, charcoal black, or dusty pink.
Use at most 4-6 colors.

# Visual style
Always use:
- Flat illustration
- No gradients
- No realistic texture
- No photographic elements
- Sharp vector shapes
- Paper-cut shapes
- Clean color blocks

# Background
Use the single solid background color that best suits the food, for example warm cream, soft beige, mustard yellow, burnt orange, dusty green, or muted teal.

# Detail rules
It should have the quality of design artwork that could hang on a wall, with the feel of a MoMA gift shop, a Scandinavian design poster, a designer fruit market print, a modern art book cover, or an editorial illustration.

# Text
Don't include any text, letters, or typography.

# Negative prompt
realistic photo, photography, camera, lens, shadow realism, food advertisement, menu design, delivery app thumbnail, glossy texture, 3D render, CGI, photorealistic, metallic effect, neon, gradient, watermark, logo, cluttered layout
```

## Observed in references

The user's original reference images aren't stored. They showed these traits, which the prompt doesn't state. Add the ones you want to the image prompt. The references also have oversized cut-paper lettering around the subject, which this style removes by the user's choice.

- **Edges:** shapes are slightly irregular and hand-cut, not perfectly geometric, with wobbly curves and angled corners.
- **Paper gaps:** thin slivers of the background often separate shapes, such as a watermelon's rind or a croissant's segments.
- **Surface:** color blocks have a faint print or paper grain, despite the prompt's "no texture" rule.
- **Depth:** a darker or lighter flat tone adds a simple side or layer, without any gradient or cast shadow.
- **Props:** related items, such as chopsticks, a plate, or leaves, appear as separate flat shapes.
- **Background:** all references use warm cream or butter tones.

## Examples

- [waffle.webp](examples/waffle.webp): conversion of a generated photo of a waffle with strawberries and butter on a plate, made with `gpt-image` from the prompt above plus a cream background, hand-cut edges, background slivers, faint grain, a kept plate, and a 70% height request. Without those additions, an earlier attempt had a teal background, smooth edges, and no plate. This result has no text and keeps the source's 3:2 ratio. Its plate is teal rather than the source's white and still spans about 90% of the height. Its slivers read like even cream outlines, and its grain looks closer to watercolor mottling than flat cut paper.
