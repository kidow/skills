# Cosmic Pixel Poster

An image reinterpreted as a vertical retro event flyer in low-resolution LED dot-matrix pixels, with a large golden title on a dark cosmic background.

## Prompt

Replace the title placeholder with the user's title. If the user gave none, ask for one.

```text
[Instruction]
Transform the uploaded reference image into a retro cosmic pixel-art event poster inspired by an old low-resolution LED dot-matrix concert flyer.

Use the uploaded image only as the main visual/theme reference. Reinterpret the subject, mood, colors, and key shapes from the image as simplified pixel-art symbols, icons, and decorative motifs. Do not create a realistic illustration. Do not preserve photographic details. Convert everything into a playful, abstract, cosmic, low-resolution poster style.

Place the main title text at the top:

"Enter the top title text here"

The title should be large, bold, blocky, and pixelated, using a retro dot-matrix / bitmap font style. The title should feel like it was drawn with small square pixels or LED dots, slightly imperfect and nostalgic.

[Overall Style]
A vertical retro event poster with a black or very dark charcoal background, filled with colorful tiny pixel stars, confetti dots, geometric shapes, small squares, rings, crosses, diamonds, moons, flowers, planets, UFO-like symbols, cactus-like silhouettes, and playful space-age icons.

The poster should feel like a mix of:
- 1980s / 1990s underground electronic music flyer
- vintage LED message board
- low-resolution arcade game screen
- cosmic pixel-art party poster
- printed zine poster with slightly rough ink texture

[Composition]
Vertical poster layout.
Large title area at the top, occupying about 25–35% of the poster height.
Below the title, arrange several small pixel-art icon sections inspired by the uploaded image.
Use the uploaded image's main subject as the central inspiration, but simplify it into pixel symbols and decorative motifs.
Create a balanced layout with open spacing, not too crowded, but filled with small colorful pixel elements across the dark background.

If the uploaded image contains a person, animal, object, product, food, landscape, or abstract scene, reinterpret it as a few symbolic pixel icons rather than a realistic drawing.
For example: turn shapes into small icons, outlines, simplified silhouettes, glowing symbols, colorful pixel clusters, or retro cosmic emblems.

[Typography]
Top title:
Large yellow or warm golden pixelated text.
Chunky bitmap-style letterforms.
Slightly uneven dot texture, like old printed pixels or an LED display.
Readable but intentionally retro and low-resolution.

Optional small subtitle/event text areas can appear lower on the poster, but keep them minimal and decorative.
Do not add random readable text unless provided.
If additional text is needed, use tiny abstract pixel-like marks, not actual words.

[Color Palette]
Dark black / charcoal background.
Main text in warm yellow, golden yellow, or muted neon yellow.
Accent colors: magenta, violet, cyan, lime green, orange, muted red, soft blue.
Use small colorful speckles and pixel confetti throughout.
Colors should feel slightly faded and printed, not glossy modern neon.

[Pixel Art Detail]
Use visible square-pixel construction.
No smooth vector shapes.
No clean modern flat design.
Edges should be jagged, blocky, and pixelated.
Icons should look like they were made on a low-resolution grid.
Add subtle halftone, screen-print grain, paper texture, and slight ink bleeding.
The result should look like a photographed or scanned vintage printed poster, not a clean digital graphic.

[Background Elements]
Scatter tiny pixel stars and colorful confetti across the entire poster.
Include simple geometric motifs:
small squares, hollow squares, circles, rings, crosses, plus signs, diamonds, crescent moons, tiny planets, tiny sparkles, abstract flowers, UFO-like shapes, and retro arcade-style symbols.
Keep the decorations playful and cosmic, but not childish.

[Image Transformation Rule]
The uploaded image should not be copied directly.
Do not keep realistic lighting, realistic textures, or photographic rendering.
Translate the image into symbolic pixel-art language.
Preserve only the conceptual identity, key colors, silhouette, mood, or theme of the uploaded image.

[Print Texture]
Add subtle paper grain, screen-printed ink texture, slight blur from old print reproduction, and mild color misregistration.
The poster should feel like a physical printed flyer photographed in a book or zine archive.

[Technical Specs]
Vertical poster.
High detail pixel-art texture.
Low-resolution aesthetic, but high-quality final image.
Sharp enough to read the title, but with intentionally rough pixel edges.
Retro underground music poster mood.
Cosmic playful atmosphere.
Dark background with colorful pixel decorations.
```

## Observed in references

The user's original reference images aren't stored. They showed these traits, which the prompt doesn't state. Add the ones you want to the image prompt.

- **Subject:** the main subject stays large, central, and recognizable as one pixel illustration, even though the prompt asks for symbolic icons. Its pose and key details carry over from the source.
- **Scene:** the source setting turns cosmic, such as a daytime window becoming a starry night city, and objects gain stars or glitter.
- **Title:** a short two-line title in all caps pairs a cosmic word with the subject.
- **Ratio:** posters are 3:4.
- **Layout:** repeated motifs from the source fill the margins, sometimes with thin dotted frames and small inset panels.
- **Source text:** logos and text in the source are dropped.
