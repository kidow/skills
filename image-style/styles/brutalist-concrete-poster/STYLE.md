# Brutalist Concrete Poster

A photo turned into a brutalist art poster: a gritty black-and-white concrete-textured image overlaid with bold flat primary color shapes, in a raw, monumental collage.

## Prompt

```text
Convert the attached photo into an original brutalist art poster, combining a black-and-white photographic layer with bold flat color shapes in a raw, graphic collage.

Photographic layer: render the photo's subject and setting in high-contrast black and white, with hard shadows and a heavy, gritty texture of rough cast concrete and coarse print grain. Emphasize strong geometry, such as edges, planes, repeated lines, and heavy shapes, so the scene feels massive and monumental. Keep the subject, setting, and composition recognizable.

Color shapes: overlay or cut in a few large, flat, solid shapes in bold colors, such as red, orange, yellow, or cobalt blue, using circles, half circles, rectangles, and thick bars. Choose one or two colors that echo the photo's main colors. Let some shapes sit behind the subject and some cross over it, and let at least one shape run off the edge of the frame. Keep them few and large, not scattered.

Composition: asymmetrical and bold, with a strong grid feeling, generous areas of flat color or empty gray, and sharp contrast between the gritty photo and the clean color shapes.

Surface: matte printed poster, with coarse halftone dots, slight ink misregistration, and paper texture. No smooth digital gradients, glossy 3D rendering, or soft painterly blending.

Don't include any text, letters, numbers, logos, or watermarks.

Keep the same aspect ratio as the attached image.
```

## Observed in references

These traits come from viewing Google image results for "brutalist art style". The images weren't stored. Add the ones you want to the image prompt. This style follows the concrete architecture art posters in those results.

- **Subjects:** most posters show concrete buildings with stacked balconies, cantilevers, and repeated windows.
- **Color:** many use a single warm accent, such as an orange sun or a red rectangle, against gray concrete.
- **Collage:** some layer cut-out photos at different scales, with diagonal stairs or towers.
- **Graphic design:** many results are type-driven posters with huge bold letters, neon colors, and dense layouts, which this style leaves out because it uses no text.
- **Architecture photos:** many results are plain photos of brutalist buildings, which this style doesn't follow.

## Examples

- [bicycle.webp](examples/bicycle.webp): conversion of a generated photo of a red bicycle against a wall, made with `gpt-image` from the prompt above. It has no text, keeps the 3:2 ratio and the source's composition, and shows a gritty black-and-white concrete wall with a large red circle, a red bar, a yellow band, and a gray triangle, with print grain and worn edges. The red circle echoes the bicycle's color while the bicycle itself became black and white. The scene is a quiet street rather than a monumental building, so it feels less massive than the references, and the poster has a slightly aged border that the prompt doesn't ask for.
