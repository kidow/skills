# Miniature on Palm

The subject of a photo shrunk into a tiny, realistic miniature resting on an open palm, in front of the photo's original background.

## Prompt

```text
Keep the subject in the attached photo as it is, but rebuild it as a tiny miniature version at about 1/50 of its real size.

- Composite the finished miniature so it rests on a person's palm (shot from a distance where the whole palm is naturally visible, without an excessive close-up of palm lines or skin texture)
- The hand enters naturally from the bottom of the photo, with the palm open
- The miniature subject is sharply in focus and, despite its tiny size, precisely reproduces the texture, color, and detail of its real materials (gloss, wrinkles, surface texture, and so on)
- Make the size ratio between the hand and the miniature extremely small (a scale smaller than a fingernail)
- Background: keep the original photo's background. Remove the full-size subject from it and naturally fill the space it occupied. Don't replace it with a sky or another setting.
- If the subject is food or a product, also place suitable miniature props, such as a plate, tray, or stand, scaled down with it
- Overall, it looks like a strong close-up taken with a macro lens, with real photographic quality (no CG feel)
- Don't include any text, captions, or arrows
- Keep the same aspect ratio as the attached image
```

## Observed in references

The user's original reference images aren't stored. They showed these traits, which the prompt doesn't state. Add the ones you want to the image prompt. The references also have a blue sky, handwritten text, and an arrow, which this style removes by the user's choice.

- **Hand:** one open hand rises from the lower left, with the palm taking up about the lower half of the frame.
- **Scale:** despite the prompt's extreme ratio, miniatures are about palm-center size, easily visible and larger than a fingernail. Sets with props can nearly cover the palm.
- **Light:** bright, direct daylight falls on the hand and the miniature, with a small contact shadow under the miniature.
- **Focus:** both the hand and the miniature stay sharp.
- **Space:** the area above the hand stays open, leaving the miniature as the clear focus.
