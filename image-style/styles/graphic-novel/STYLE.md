# Graphic Novel

A photo redrawn as a single panel from a modern graphic novel, with confident ink line work, heavy black shadows, flat colors, and cinematic lighting.

## Prompt

```text
Convert the attached photo into a single original illustration in the style of a modern graphic novel panel. Avoid copying any existing comic characters, costumes, or artists.

Ink: draw everything with confident, hand-inked black lines that vary from thick outer contours to fine interior lines. Use bold spot blacks for deep shadows, and add a few feathered hatching strokes where light turns into shadow.

Color: flat or lightly graded digital comic coloring with a limited, moody palette based on the photo's colors, such as warm ambers against cool teals or blues. Shadows are clearly separated color shapes, not soft gradients, and a few highlights pick out the main light source.

Lighting: dramatic, cinematic light with strong contrast, keeping the photo's light direction and time of day.

Subject: keep the photo's people, poses, expressions, clothing, setting, and composition, drawn with semi-realistic proportions and expressive faces. Simplify textures into clean shapes, and frame the scene like a film still. Don't add people, objects, or costumes that aren't in the photo.

Surface: crisp printed comic art with a very slight paper or print texture. No photographic detail, painterly brushwork, 3D rendering, or anime styling.

Show a single image only, with no panel borders, gutters, speech balloons, captions, sound effects, or other text, logos, or watermarks.

Keep the same aspect ratio as the attached image.
```

## Observed in references

These traits come from viewing Google image results for "graphic novel art style". The images weren't stored. Add the ones you want to the image prompt.

- **Noir:** some use heavy black shadows with muted teal and amber color for a gritty mood.
- **Painted color:** some semi-realistic covers use painted, highly detailed coloring with strong light, closer to illustration.
- **Clean line:** some use thin, clean lines with flat bright color, closer to European or webcomic styles.
- **Pages:** many results show multi-panel pages with speech balloons and captions, which this style leaves out.
- **Superheroes:** results also include superhero covers and characters, which this style doesn't follow.
