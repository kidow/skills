# Junji Ito

A photo redrawn as a black-and-white horror manga panel in the style of Junji Ito, with fine, realistic pen lines, dense hatching, wide staring eyes, long flowing strands of hair, and an eerie, unsettling calm.

## Prompt

```text
Convert the attached photo into an original black-and-white horror manga illustration in the style of Junji Ito. Don't copy any specific existing Junji Ito page, panel, or character, such as Tomie or the Uzumaki spiral girl.

Color: pure black ink on white paper only, with no gray wash, screentone gradients, or color.

Line: realistic, finely observed pen work with thin, precise contour lines, and dense parallel hatching and crosshatching for every shadow and texture. Build dark areas from many fine lines rather than flat fills, and use solid black only for the deepest shadows and dark hair.

Faces: draw faces realistically and calmly, with clear features and natural proportions, but with an uncanny stillness. Make the eyes slightly wide with detailed irises, small pupils, and fine lashes, and add faint lines under the eyes, as if the people are quietly unaware of something wrong.

Hair: render hair as many long, separate, flowing ink strands with glossy black areas and thin white highlight lines.

Atmosphere: make the scene feel quietly ominous. Fill parts of the background and shadows with subtle, obsessive patterns of fine concentric or spiraling lines and dense organic hatching, while keeping the photo's setting recognizable. Don't add monsters, blood, gore, or body distortion.

Subject: keep the photo's people, poses, expressions, clothing, objects, and composition recognizable. Don't add people, objects, or costumes that aren't in the photo.

Surface: a clean, hand-inked manga page drawing on white paper. No photographic detail, digital gradients, or 3D rendering.

Show only the illustration filling the frame, as a single panel with no panel borders, speech balloons, sound effects, or page layout.

Don't include any text, signature, or watermark.

Keep the same aspect ratio as the attached image.
```

## Observed in references

These traits come from viewing Google image results for "Junji Ito style" from the user's link. The images weren't stored. Add the ones you want to the image prompt.

- **Subjects:** most results are close-ups of young women with long straight black hair and blunt bangs, staring with wide eyes.
- **Spirals:** many show concentric spirals, in the background, in hair, or replacing an eye or face, which this style keeps only as subtle background patterns.
- **Body horror:** some show faces splitting, peeling, or covered in eyes or holes, with grotesque creatures, which this style leaves out unless the user asks for it.
- **Contrast:** the calmer pieces show clean, pretty faces drawn with light lines on white, while the scary ones fill the frame with dense black hatching.
- **Pages:** some results show full manga pages with panels and sound effects, and fan sketch sheets of many faces, which this style leaves out.
- **Color:** a few covers use a single muted teal or red with black ink, but nearly all results are black and white.
- **Overlap:** `graphic-novel` uses bold ink with flat moody colors and cinematic light, and `etching-engraving` uses intaglio line prints, while this style uses fine manga pen lines, wide eyes, flowing hair, and quiet horror.
