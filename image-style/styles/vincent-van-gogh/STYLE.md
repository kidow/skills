# Vincent van Gogh

In the style of Dutch Post-Impressionist painter Vincent van Gogh: a photo repainted in thick, swirling, directional brushstrokes with deep, earthy complementary colors and bold outlines.

## Prompt

```text
Convert the attached photo into an original oil painting in the Post-Impressionist style of Vincent van Gogh. Avoid copying any specific existing artwork, and don't add a starry sky, cypress trees, or sunflowers unless they're in the photo.

Brushwork: cover the entire canvas with thick, clearly separated strokes of impasto oil paint, with visible ridges. Use large, irregular strokes of varying length and pressure, not a uniform stamped pattern. Strokes are directional and rhythmic: they follow the form of each object and flow, twist, and swirl around it, filling the background with a sense of movement. Change the stroke direction and length for each surface, such as curving strokes for sky and foliage, and long parallel strokes for walls, ground, and clothing.

Color: deep, earthy, expressive oil colors rather than natural color, with lowered brightness and saturation, such as ultramarine, Prussian blue, ochre, olive green, deep red, and umber, like aged oil paint. Use only small accents of bright yellow. Replace pale cream and white areas with darker ochre, muted gold, and gray-blue, so the mood stays rich and heavy, not bright or cheerful. Place complementary colors side by side so they reinforce each other, such as blue against orange, yellow against purple, and red against green. Keep the subject's main colors recognizable, and let the shadows and background carry the contrasts.

Line: use bold, dark blue or black painted outlines around the main shapes, like a Japanese woodblock print, drawn with visible brushstrokes.

Composition: keep the photo's subject, setting, and arrangement, with slightly wobbly, hand-painted forms that aren't mechanically accurate, and flattened space. Paint shiny surfaces such as metal with a few loose strokes of dull color, with no realistic highlights. Don't add major objects that aren't in the photo. For people, keep their features and clothing, modeled with visible colored strokes on the face.

Surface: a thick oil painting on canvas, with no photographic detail, digital smoothness, or blended gradients.

Don't include any text, signature, or watermark.

Keep the same aspect ratio as the attached image.
```

## Observed in references

These traits come from viewing Google image results for Van Gogh's style. The images weren't stored. Add the ones you want to the image prompt.

- **Palette:** deep ultramarine and cobalt blue with chrome yellow dominate the most recognized works, often together.
- **Portraits:** faces are built from short, directional strokes of green, orange, and blue, against a swirling or patterned background.
- **Skies:** skies are full of concentric swirls around light sources.
- **Calmer works:** blossom and still-life paintings have flat, pale backgrounds, clearer outlines, and a more decorative feel.
- **AI imitations:** the results also include AI images with a swirl filter over modern scenes, which look busier and more uniform than the real works.
