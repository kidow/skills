# Gustav Klimt

A photo repainted in the manner of Gustav Klimt's Golden Phase, with naturally modeled faces and hands set inside flat, shimmering fields of gold leaf and dense ornamental patterns.

## Prompt

```text
Convert the attached photo into an original painting in the style of Gustav Klimt's Golden Phase (around 1899–1910), Vienna Secession and Art Nouveau. Don't copy any specific existing Klimt painting.

Contrast of flat and round: paint faces, hands, and other bare skin naturally and softly modeled, with pale, delicate skin tones. Everything else, such as clothing, bodies under clothing, objects, and background, becomes flat, two-dimensional decorative surfaces with almost no shading or perspective.

Gold: use real gold and bronze leaf as the dominant material, with a slightly uneven, shimmering metallic surface, fine gold flecks, and areas of raised gilded relief. Backgrounds become flat gold fields with scattered gold speckles, or dense mosaic-like meadows of small flowers.

Ornament: fill clothing and large shapes with tightly packed patterns, such as gold spirals, concentric circles, eye-like ovals, small black and white rectangles, checkerboards, triangles, and flower clusters, like a Byzantine mosaic. Use different pattern sets for different people or objects, and let the patterns follow each shape's outline.

Color: gold, ochre, and bronze dominate, with jewel-like accents of deep red, emerald green, turquoise, violet, and black, plus pale ivory skin.

Subject: keep the photo's subject, setting, composition, poses, and expressions recognizable. Simplify complex objects into flat golden silhouettes decorated with patterns. Don't add people, objects, or costumes that aren't in the photo.

Surface: oil paint and gold leaf on canvas, with a matte, slightly aged finish. No photographic detail, digital gradients, or 3D rendering.

Show only the painting filling the frame, with no frame, wall, or border.

Don't include any text, signature, or watermark.

Keep the same aspect ratio as the attached image.
```

## Observed in references

These traits come from viewing Google image results for "gustav klimt paintings" and from the sources below. The images weren't stored. Add the ones you want to the image prompt.

- **Portraits:** most famous works are portraits of women in long robes, where the head and hands float above a robe that dissolves into gold pattern.
- **Couples:** embracing couples are wrapped in one shared golden cloak, with rectangles for one figure and circles and flowers for the other.
- **Tree of Life:** some works use large spiraling golden branches across the whole background.
- **Landscapes:** Klimt also painted square landscapes of dense, pointillist foliage, flower meadows, and lake villages, without gold. This style follows the Golden Phase instead.
- **Formats:** many paintings are tall portraits or squares, which this style doesn't follow, since it keeps the source's ratio.
- **Overlap:** `dancheong` and `woven-tapestry` also use dense patterns, but this style is the only one that combines realistic faces with flat gold leaf ornament.
