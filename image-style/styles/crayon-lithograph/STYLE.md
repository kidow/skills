# Crayon Lithograph

A photo redrawn as a hand-pulled stone lithograph, with grainy greasy-crayon tones in black and one or two flat printed color layers on cream paper.

## Prompt

```text
Convert the attached photo into an original hand-pulled stone lithograph, like 19th- and early 20th-century lithographic prints. Avoid copying any specific existing artwork.

Drawing: draw everything as if with a greasy lithographic crayon on stone. Build tones from soft, grainy crayon texture, where the stone's grain breaks the strokes into fine speckles, from light gray to rich black. Use loose, confident crayon contours rather than thin pen lines, and leave the brightest areas as bare paper.

Color: print in black plus one or two flat color layers, chosen from the photo's main colors, such as a warm red, an ochre, or a soft blue. Each color is a flat, slightly uneven area with crayon-textured edges, not a full-color painting, and the color layers may sit slightly out of register with the black drawing. Add a little fine ink spatter in a few areas for texture.

Subject: keep the photo's subject, setting, composition, and recognizable details, simplified into bold shapes with clear silhouettes, and let less important areas fade into lightly drawn or bare paper.

Surface: printed on warm cream paper with a soft, matte, slightly uneven ink look. No photographic detail, digital gradients, smooth airbrushing, or hatching built from fine engraved lines.

Don't include any text, lettering, signature, border, or watermark.

Keep the same aspect ratio as the attached image.
```

## Observed in references

These traits come from viewing Google image results for "lithography art". The images weren't stored. Add the ones you want to the image prompt.

- **Monochrome prints:** many are black-and-white crayon lithographs of landscapes, storms, and figures, with soft, grainy tones and deep blacks.
- **Color posters:** Belle Époque posters use large flat color shapes, bold black silhouettes, and spatter texture, often with lettering, which this style leaves out.
- **Ornate posters:** some Art Nouveau posters have decorative halos, flowing hair, and floral borders, which this style doesn't follow.
- **Modern prints:** results also include modern abstract color lithographs and photos of the printing process.
- **Overlap:** `etching-engraving` builds tone from fine lines, while this style builds tone from grainy crayon texture.
