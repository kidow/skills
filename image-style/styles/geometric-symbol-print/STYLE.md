# Geometric Symbol Print

A subject distilled into one abstract geometric symbol, printed in a few flat inks on warm paper with fine construction lines.

## Prompt

```text
Turn each uploaded photo into its own standalone design print with the same aspect ratio as the photo, without mixing different photos. The output is only the abstract print. Don't include the original photo or a split layout. If the input is a screenshot of a finished piece, extract only the work and remove interface elements, watermarks, and page text.

Distill a self-contained abstract visual symbol from the photo. First identify the two or three most recognizable traits: silhouette, structure, movement, repeating rhythm, or narrative relationship. Then compress them using a few geometric modules, lines, cuts, overlaps, and negative space. Capture the subject's character and relationships without reproducing the whole object. Don't draw a complete landscape or illustration, and don't force different subjects into the same kind of shape.

Use the language of post-digital graphic design, deconstructed Swiss design, generative symbol systems, and diagrammatic abstraction. The main symbol is the only visual focus, placed at the center or slightly off-center, with generous empty space. Add only a very few arc trajectories, partial construction grids, thin lines, abstract nodes, and tiny marks where they have structural meaning. Don't pile up icons or arrange them like an infographic.

Extract one or two signature colors and one supporting color from the photo, and pair them with a warm white or off-white ground. Color separates modules and relationships. Use independent flat print colors and restrained overprinting. Avoid messy palettes, cheap neon, and commercial gradients. Add very faint stencil-print grain, photocopy or scan noise, paper fibers, and local print misregistration, with slightly imperfect ink edges. No heavy stains, cracks, or uniform distressing.

Text is limited to tiny, readable numbers, letters, or short indexes tied to specific modules. Use no main title, filler copy, or garbled text. The overall feel is restrained and spare, combining precise order, slight handmade error, and cultural memory. Forbidden: templated logos, commercial UI, cyberpunk, heavy futurism, random piles of geometry, decorative patterns, dense annotation, and watermarks.
```

## Observed in references

The user's original reference images aren't stored. They showed these traits, which the prompt doesn't state. Add the ones you want to the image prompt.

- **Abstraction:** subjects reduce to a few bold shapes, such as a cat as a ring of crescents or a staircase as curved wedges around a dark circle. Some, like a yellow character, stay more recognizable.
- **Color:** the palette comes directly from the photo, such as terracotta, cherry pink with olive, or mustard with olive. A small dark circle sometimes acts as an anchor.
- **Overlap:** translucent layers darken where they cross.
- **Annotation:** one thin arc with small pink or red nodes and short crosshair ticks surrounds the symbol. Tiny `01` and `02` labels in monospace connect by thin leader lines, or a single index like `A-203` sits to one side.
- **Misregistration:** a faint pink edge sometimes shows along one side of a shape.

## Examples

- [park-kite.webp](examples/park-kite.webp): conversion of a generated photo of a person flying a kite with a dog near a house, made with `gpt-image` from the prompt above. Its colors come from the source, and its grain, arcs, and index labels match the references. It keeps the whole scene as several small figures instead of one abstract symbol, so ask for stronger abstraction and a single focus when needed.
