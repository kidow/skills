# Colored Pencil Sketch

A photo redrawn as a loose colored pencil sketch on white paper, with layered short strokes that scatter into bare paper at the edges.

## Prompt

```text
Turn the attached photo into an emotive sketch illustration drawn in colored pencil, according to the rules below.

[Style]
A free hand drawing that looks quickly drawn with thick colored pencils on white paper. Express form and color by layering short, rough lines many times. The coloring direction may vary a little, and lines should naturally stray outside the contours, with the white of the paper showing through here and there.

Make it look like a sketchbook drawing done spontaneously on location rather than a precisely finished digital illustration. Don't use smooth gradients, realistic photographic texture, crisp vector outlines, or 3D rendering.

[Main subject]
Make the most important subject in the photo the center of the drawing. Whether it's food, a product, a person, an animal, or a landscape, keep the features that make the original recognizable. Preserve the face and hairstyle for a person, fur color and markings for an animal, shape and main colors for food and products, and terrain and building layout for a landscape.

Boldly reduce fine detail, and express the characteristic forms with strong colored pencil lines and areas of color. Don't create new major objects that aren't in the photo.

[Composition]
Place the subject naturally at the center of the frame, but don't make it look like a precisely symmetrical poster. Don't cut the drawing's edges straight; let the colored pencil lines gradually scatter and fade into the white paper.

Leave generous white margins at the paper's edges. Don't fill the entire background without gaps; draw loosely only the parts needed to understand the subject.

[Line and color]
Establish the main forms with dark lines, and layer bright colors in short strokes for liveliness. Choose a vivid but natural colored pencil palette based on the main colors of the original photo. Leave the white of the paper visible throughout the drawing, and don't color with overly soft blending.

[Mood]
A fresh, relaxed mood, as if drawn in a sketchbook while traveling in the sunlight. Keep the charm of slightly rough, unfinished-looking lines, while keeping the subject clearly recognizable.

[Text]
Don't include letters, titles, signatures, dates, numbers, logos, or watermarks.

[Output]
Generate only one finished drawing. The white paper with the drawing fills the frame, with no frame, wall-hung mockup, open sketchbook, or scene with colored pencil tools.

[Ratio]
Same as the attached image.
```

## Observed in references

The user's original reference images aren't stored. They showed these traits, which the prompt doesn't state. Add the ones you want to the image prompt.

- **Line weight:** strokes are fine and dense rather than thick, like many thin pencil lines scribbled over each other.
- **Detail:** subjects stay fairly detailed and solid, with focal areas such as eyes, faces, and food carefully rendered, while the surroundings stay loose.
- **Background:** diagonal zigzag scribbles in one or two colors, often blue sky and green, form a rough vignette around the subject.
- **Dark subjects:** black fur or dark areas build from dense layered lines with small highlights left bare.
- **Scenes:** a detailed scene can fill most of the page, with its strokes thinning out only near the paper's edges.
- **Ratio:** all references are 3:4.
