# Sepia Vintage

A photo redrawn as an antique sepia artwork, with fine brown ink lines and soft monochrome brown washes on aged, stained cream paper that fades softly toward the edges.

## Prompt

```text
Convert the attached photo into an original antique sepia artwork, like a 19th-century ink and wash drawing.

Color: monochrome only, in warm sepia browns, from deep umber and burnt sienna in the darkest areas through mid browns and soft tan to the pale cream of the paper in the highlights. No other colors.

Line: draw the main contours and details with fine, delicate brown ink lines of varying weight, with light hatching and crosshatching in the shadows, like a skilled illustrator's pen work.

Wash: shade the forms with soft, transparent layers of brown ink wash, with gentle gradations, slightly uneven pooling, and soft edges, built up in a few layers for depth.

Focus: render the subject with the most detail and contrast, and let the background become lighter, softer, and more faded, with some areas left as loose wash or bare paper.

Paper: aged cream or parchment paper with a warm yellowed tone, visible fibers and grain, faint foxing spots, light water stains, and a soft darkening vignette toward the edges.

Subject: keep the photo's subject, setting, composition, poses, and expressions recognizable and realistically drawn. Don't add objects or costumes that aren't in the photo.

Surface: hand-drawn ink and wash on old paper, slightly faded and timeworn. No photographic detail, digital filters, or color tints other than sepia.

Show only the artwork filling the frame, with no frame, mat, wall, room, or border.

Don't include any text, labels, stamps, signature, or watermark.

Keep the same aspect ratio as the attached image.
```

## Observed in references

These traits come from viewing Google image results for "sepia vintage art" from the user's link. The images weren't stored. Add the ones you want to the image prompt.

- **Subjects:** pastoral landscapes with trees and rivers, botanical studies, animals such as frogs, snails, and octopuses, ships, and classical figure drawings dominate.
- **Media:** most look like brown ink drawings, ink and wash, or faded old oil landscapes, with a few toned old photographs.
- **Paper:** many show aged parchment with stains, foxing, and soft vignettes.
- **Context:** many results show framed prints and murals in living rooms, which this style leaves out.
- **Labels:** some botanical and specimen prints have handwritten labels or stamps, which this style leaves out.
- **Overlap:** `etching-engraving` uses black intaglio lines, and `graphite-pencil-sketch` uses gray pencil, while this style uses brown ink lines and washes on aged paper.
