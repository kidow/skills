# Pablo Picasso

In the style of Spanish painter Pablo Picasso's late 1930s portraits: figures painted with combined profile and frontal faces, thick black curved outlines, flat bold colors, and patterned clothing.

## Prompt

```text
Convert the attached photo into an original oil painting in the style of Pablo Picasso's portraits from the late 1930s. Avoid copying any specific existing artwork.

Faces: for each person, combine a profile and a front view in the same face. Show the nose in profile while both eyes face the viewer, place the eyes at different heights, and give the face two contrasting flat colors split by a curving line, such as yellow and green, or pink and violet. Keep each person's hairstyle, accessories, and expression recognizable.

Figures: reshape bodies and hands with simplified, rounded, slightly distorted forms, keeping each person's pose and action from the photo. The people are the focus of the painting.

Line: thick, black, painted outlines with confident curves around every shape, with visible brushwork and slight unevenness.

Color: flat, saturated, unblended areas of bold color, with sharp contrasts between neighboring areas, such as red against green, yellow against violet, and blue against orange. Keep the main clothing colors from the photo, but feel free to change skin and shadow colors expressively.

Pattern: fill clothing and some background areas with bold painted stripes, plaids, crosshatching, or dots.

Background: simplify the setting into a few large flat color areas with minimal detail, and keep only objects from the photo that matter. The space is flat and shallow, with no realistic perspective or shading.

Surface: oil paint on canvas with visible, slightly rough brush texture. No photographic detail, smooth gradients, or 3D rendering.

Don't include any text, signature, or watermark.

Keep the same aspect ratio as the attached image.
```

## Observed in references

These traits come from viewing Google image results for Picasso's style. The images weren't stored. Add the ones you want to the image prompt. This style follows the late 1930s portraits that dominate those results.

- **Seated women:** many portraits show a single seated woman in an armchair, with hands near the face.
- **Hats and hair:** women wear colorful hats, and hair is painted as long flat strokes in green, black, or red.
- **Other periods:** the results also include Blue Period works in monochrome blue, Rose Period harlequins, brown-gray Analytic Cubism, and black-and-white works with gray tones.
- **Late portraits:** some later portraits use bold black-and-white faces with flat red or blue backgrounds.
