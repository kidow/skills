# Amedeo Modigliani

A photo repainted as an Amedeo Modigliani-style portrait painting, with elongated mask-like faces, long swan necks, almond eyes, simple curving outlines, and warm, earthy colors against plain backgrounds.

## Prompt

```text
Convert the attached photo into an original oil painting in the style of Amedeo Modigliani (School of Paris, 1915–1919). Don't copy any specific existing Modigliani painting.

Faces: stretch faces into long, smooth ovals, like simplified carved masks. Give them long, narrow, slightly hooked noses drawn with one line, small closed mouths, and narrow almond-shaped eyes that are often filled with a single flat color, such as blue, gray, or dark brown, with little or no pupil. Tilt heads slightly to one side. Soften the photo's expressions into calm, quiet, slightly melancholic ones, while keeping each person recognizable by hair, build, and features.

Bodies: elongate necks into long, smooth columns, and make shoulders narrow and gently sloping. Simplify bodies, arms, and hands into graceful, flowing shapes with smooth, curving outlines.

Line: draw contours with confident, flowing dark lines in brown, black, or dark blue, especially around faces, necks, and shoulders.

Color: warm, earthy palette based on the photo's colors, with ochre, apricot, and terracotta skin with soft rosy cheeks, deep reds, rust, burnt orange, dark blues, and black for clothing, and muted gray-blue or brown for backgrounds.

Background: simplify the photo's setting into a few flat, muted planes of color, such as a plain wall, a door, or a simple angled floor, with loose, visible brushwork. Keep it calm and uncluttered so the figures dominate.

Subject: keep the photo's people, poses, clothing colors, objects, and composition recognizable. Paint objects in the same simplified shapes and warm colors. Don't add people or objects that aren't in the photo.

Surface: oil paint on canvas with thin, slightly dry layers, soft visible brushstrokes, and a matte finish. No photographic detail, digital gradients, or 3D rendering.

Show only the painting filling the frame, with no frame, wall, or border.

Don't include any text, signature, name, or watermark.

Keep the same aspect ratio as the attached image.
```

## Observed in references

These traits come from viewing Google image results for "amedeo modigliani portrait paintings" and from the sources below. The images weren't stored. Add the ones you want to the image prompt.

- **Portraits:** almost every work is a single seated or half-length figure facing the viewer, with hands folded in the lap.
- **Eyes:** some eyes are fully blue or gray with no pupils, while others are drawn with small dark pupils, and some faces have one blank eye.
- **Hair:** hair is often parted in the middle or pulled up into a simple bun, in reddish brown or black.
- **Names:** some paintings have the sitter's name or a signature painted near the top, which this style leaves out.
- **Formats:** most paintings are tall portraits, which this style doesn't follow, since it keeps the source's ratio.
- **Overlap:** `pablo-picasso` combines profile and frontal views with bold flat colors, while this style keeps a single view with elongated, calm forms and warm earth tones.
