# Retro-Futurism

A photo reimagined as a 1950s–60s space-age illustration of the future, with streamlined chrome forms, warm teal and orange colors, and vintage print texture.

## Prompt

```text
Convert the attached photo into an original retro-futurist illustration, imagining the scene as the future was pictured in 1950s and 1960s science fiction magazines and space-age advertising.

Redesign: keep the photo's subject, setting, composition, and main colors recognizable, but reimagine man-made objects as space-age designs, with streamlined curves, rounded chrome trim, tail fins, bubble domes, glowing lights, and atomic-age details. Reimagine buildings with sleek curves, round windows, and futuristic lines. Don't add rockets, robots, planets, people, or other major objects that aren't in the photo.

Painting: a hand-painted gouache and airbrush illustration with smooth, idealized forms, crisp edges, and polished highlights on chrome and glass. Lighting is bright and optimistic, with a warm glow.

Color: a mid-century palette of teal, turquoise, orange, coral, mustard yellow, cream, and deep navy, with saturated but slightly faded colors.

Sky and setting: turn any sky into a clean gradient from cream or pale yellow to teal or deep blue, and keep the photo's time of day.

Surface: vintage printed matter, with fine halftone dots, slight paper grain, and gentle print misregistration. No photographic detail, modern 3D rendering, or neon cyberpunk look.

Don't include any text, logos, or watermarks.

Keep the same aspect ratio as the attached image.
```

## Observed in references

These traits come from viewing Google image results for "retro futurism style". The images weren't stored. Add the ones you want to the image prompt. This style follows the illustration approach in those results.

- **Space scenes:** many illustrations show astronauts, robots, and planets with ringed orbits and starry skies.
- **Cities:** domed and spired cities glow at night, often with flying cars and monorails.
- **Flat poster versions:** some are flat, minimal posters with a large sun or moon and simple shapes.
- **Fashion and interiors:** many results are space-age fashion and 1960s–70s interiors with curved furniture and orange tones, which this style doesn't follow.
- **Y2K versions:** a few show chrome and neon Y2K designs, also not followed.

## Examples

- [bicycle.webp](examples/bicycle.webp): conversion of a generated photo of a red bicycle against a wall, made with `gpt-image` from the prompt above. It has no text, keeps the 3:2 ratio and the source's composition, and redesigns the bicycle with tail fins, chrome trim, glowing lights, and a bubble dome over the seat. The wall became a curved space-age building with porthole windows, and the scene has warm gouache light and vintage print texture. It reads closer to an advertising illustration than to the references' space scenes, and the leaf shadows and flowers stay naturalistic.
