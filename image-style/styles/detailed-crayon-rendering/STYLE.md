# Detailed Crayon Rendering

A subject redrawn faithfully with dense, waxy crayon strokes, keeping its form and detail, with a warm, innocent feel and no text or doodles.

## Prompt

```text
Analyze the uploaded image and identify the main subject (food, object, person, or animal).

[Core goal]
Transform the image into a crayon illustration on a sketchbook page, drawn with a young child's warm feeling.

Important:
The entire image must be reinterpreted in a crayon drawing style.
Do not keep photorealistic textures.

The result must look:
- Fully drawn with crayons, including the main subject
- Warm, emotional, slightly messy, and innocent

[Subject reinterpretation - very important]
- The original subject must be redrawn in crayon style
- Do not paste or keep the original photo texture
- Convert all elements (food, plate, background) into crayon illustration

But:
- Preserve the subject's core shape, structure, and composition
- Keep it clearly recognizable
- Maintain the original layout and proportions

[Crayon rendering style - critical]
Apply this to the entire image:
- Many fine crayon strokes densely layered to build carefully detailed forms
- Visible texture (wax-like, grainy fill)
- Slight color overlap and imperfect coloring
- A hand-colored feeling (not clean digital shading)
- No realistic gradients

[Not included]
Don't add text, handwriting, titles, numbers, stick figures, smiling faces, hearts, stars, suns, clouds, arrows, circles, scribbles, or other doodles that aren't in the image. Don't draw spiral binding or a notebook.

[Overall style]
- Fully illustrated (no photorealism)
- Everything unified in one consistent style

[Final output]
- A fully crayon-drawn image
- No real photo elements remaining
- Subject and environment in the same crayon style

[Ratio]
Keep the same aspect ratio as the uploaded image.
```

## Observed in references

The user's original reference images aren't stored. They showed these traits, which the prompt doesn't state. Add the ones you want to the image prompt. The references also have handwritten Korean diary text, child doodles, and spiral binding, which this style removes by the user's choice.

- **Paper:** warm cream paper with a slight tooth shows through the wax.
- **Filling:** subjects are densely colored with almost no gaps, while the surrounding table or plate is looser.
- **Outlines:** dark, sketchy crayon lines define shapes without being uniform.
- **Color:** colors stay bright and saturated, with visible strokes of a second hue layered on top.
- **Detail:** small details such as patterns on dishes and pieces of food stay readable.
