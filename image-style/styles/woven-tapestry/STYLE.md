# Woven Tapestry

A photo rewoven as an antique European wall tapestry, with visible wool threads, faded natural-dye colors, flat perspective, and dense foliage.

## Prompt

```text
Convert the attached photo into a photograph of an antique hand-woven wall tapestry in the style of European medieval and Renaissance tapestries. Avoid copying any specific existing artwork.

Weave: the whole image is made of woven wool and silk threads. Show the visible horizontal weft and vertical warp texture, slightly fuzzy fibers, and small stepped edges where colors meet, like a real tapestry. No brushstrokes, smooth digital color, or printed image.

Color: a limited palette of faded natural-dye colors, such as indigo blue, muted green, ochre and gold, cream, and soft madder red, with gently aged and uneven tones. Shade forms with bands of hatching in two or three tones of the same thread color, not smooth gradients.

Drawing: simplified, slightly stiff figures and objects with clear dark outlines and a flat, decorative sense of space. Stack depth vertically rather than with realistic perspective.

Setting: keep the photo's subject, setting, composition, and main colors recognizable. Turn plants and greenery from the photo into dense, decorative leaves and flowers that fill the surrounding space, but don't add major objects, animals, or people that aren't in the photo.

Surface: show it as a flat textile viewed straight on and filling the frame, with soft even light that reveals the thread texture. No border, frame, fringe, wall, or room around it.

Don't include any text, letters, or watermark.

Keep the same aspect ratio as the attached image.
```

## Observed in references

These traits come from viewing Google image results for "tapestry style". The images weren't stored. Most results show tapestries hanging in interiors. Add the ones you want to the image prompt.

- **Verdure:** many tapestries are dense blue-green forests and gardens with leaves larger than life, in a dark, muted palette.
- **Millefleurs:** some figures stand on a red or dark ground scattered with countless small flowers and animals.
- **Embroidered narrative:** a few show linen with simple stitched figures and lettering, closer to embroidery than woven tapestry.
- **Borders:** many antique tapestries have wide ornamental borders of flowers and scrolls, which this style leaves out.
- **Modern versions:** results also include fashion, wallpaper, and printed bohemian wall hangings, which this style doesn't follow.
