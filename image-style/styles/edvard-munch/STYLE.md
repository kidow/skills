# Edvard Munch

A photo repainted as an Edvard Munch-style Expressionist painting, with long flowing wave-like lines, simplified mask-like figures, and intense, unnatural colors in thin, rough oil paint that create an uneasy mood.

## Prompt

```text
Convert the attached photo into an original painting in the style of Edvard Munch (Norwegian Expressionism and Symbolism, 1890s–1910s). Don't copy any specific existing Munch painting, such as a screaming figure on a bridge.

Line: shape the whole scene with long, flowing, wave-like brushstrokes that follow and echo the contours of the land, sky, water, walls, and figures. Let the sky and background ripple in parallel curving bands, and let strong diagonals, such as roads or railings, pull sharply into the distance.

Color: intense, emotional, non-naturalistic colors based on the photo's colors, such as blood-red, orange, and yellow skies, deep Prussian blues and violets, acid greens, and murky browns and blacks. Use a few strong color contrasts rather than many small colors.

Figures: simplify people into flat, solid silhouettes with dark outlines and few details. Faces become pale, slightly mask-like, with simplified features and hollow, shadowed eyes, while keeping the photo's expressions. Clothing becomes dark or strongly colored flat masses. Add long, dark shadows that stretch away from the figures.

Mood: make the scene feel tense, lonely, and charged with emotion, like twilight or the pale Nordic summer night, even if the photo is cheerful.

Paint: thin, diluted oil paint applied in loose, rough, fast strokes, with drips, scratches, and bare canvas showing through in places, so the painting looks raw and unfinished. No thick impasto, fine detail, or smooth blending.

Subject: keep the photo's subject, setting, composition, poses, and expressions recognizable. Don't add people, objects, or costumes that aren't in the photo.

Show only the painting filling the frame, with no frame, wall, or border.

Don't include any text, signature, or watermark.

Keep the same aspect ratio as the attached image.
```

## Observed in references

These traits come from viewing Google image results for "edvard munch paintings" and from the sources below. The images weren't stored. Add the ones you want to the image prompt.

- **Skies:** many works have wavy orange, red, and yellow sunset skies over dark blue fjords.
- **Figures:** people often stand along a diagonal railing or road, with pale, ghostly faces, dark coats, and hats.
- **Interiors:** sickrooms and bedrooms are painted in murky greens and blues, with rough, scratched surfaces.
- **Nights:** some scenes show moonlit shores with a single column of moonlight reflected in the water.
- **Prints:** Munch also made black-and-white woodcuts and etchings with wood-grain lines, which this style doesn't follow.
- **Overlap:** `vincent-van-gogh` uses thick, swirling impasto with bright complementary colors, while this style uses thin, rough paint, long flowing lines, and an anxious mood.
