# Etching Engraving

A photo redrawn as a black-and-white intaglio etching or engraving, with every tone built from fine parallel lines, crosshatching, and stipple on white paper.

## Prompt

```text
Convert the attached photo into an original black-and-white etching in the style of traditional intaglio prints and engraved illustrations.

Line work: build every form and tone only from fine black ink lines on white paper. Use parallel hatching for light shading, crosshatching in two or three directions for darker areas, and tiny stipple dots for soft transitions. Lines follow the contours of each form, curving around round shapes and running straight along flat surfaces, like a burin or etching needle. Leave highlights as clean white paper.

Contrast: strong tonal range from pure white to dense black, with deep shadows packed with crosshatching and crisp outlines that vary in weight.

Detail: keep the photo's subject, setting, composition, and details recognizable, rendered precisely with careful, consistent line spacing. The subject has the most detail, while distant background areas use sparser, lighter lines.

Surface: the look of an ink print on warm off-white paper, with slightly irregular ink lines and faint plate tone. No color, gray washes, smooth gradients, photographic texture, or digital filter look.

Don't include any text, signature, border, or watermark.

Keep the same aspect ratio as the attached image.
```

## Observed in references

These traits come from viewing Google image results for "etching style". The images weren't stored. Add the ones you want to the image prompt.

- **Portraits:** many show faces built from dense contour lines that wrap around the forms, like banknote engravings, with high contrast and hair as flowing line groups.
- **Isolated subjects:** many results show a single object, animal, or person on white, with no background.
- **Antique prints:** old prints have warm cream or yellowed paper and softer, sketchier lines.
- **Tattoo versions:** many results are etching-style tattoos on skin, which this style doesn't follow.
- **Woodcut versions:** some use thicker woodcut lines, such as stylized waves, which are bolder than etching.

## Examples

- [bicycle.webp](examples/bicycle.webp): conversion of a generated photo of a red bicycle against a wall, made with `gpt-image` from the prompt above. It has no text, border, or color, keeps the 3:2 ratio and the source's composition, and renders the bicycle, flowers, cobblestones, and leaf shadows in fine hatching and crosshatching on warm paper. The wall's shadows read slightly gray and soft rather than purely line-built, and the overall contrast is gentler than the references' dense black portraits.
