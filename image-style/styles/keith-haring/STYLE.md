# Keith Haring

In the style of American artist Keith Haring: a photo reinterpreted as a dense all-over pop pattern of simplified figures and symbols, with thick black outlines and flat primary colors.

## Prompt

```text
Convert the attached photo into the graphic style below. Reinterpret its subject and main elements as simplified figures and symbolic forms within the pattern, keeping the subject recognizable.

Keith Haring-inspired graphic transformation, with an original composition that doesn't copy any specific artwork.

Line: the line is the main element. Use bold black contour lines of nearly even weight, drawn by hand in one quick, confident, rhythmic motion, with rounded ends and organic curves rather than clean geometric vector shapes.

Figures and subject: simplified people, animals, and symbolic forms read like pictograms or hieroglyphs. People are round-headed, faceless outlined silhouettes with no eyes, mouth, hair, clothing, or skin tone, in dynamic dancing poses that interact with the subject. Draw the photo's subject as a similarly simple pictogram. Don't add rainbows, flowers, birds, cats, or other decorative objects that aren't in the photo.

Energy: short black dashes radiate around figures and symbols to show motion and energy.

Color: solid flat fills in saturated red, blue, and yellow, plus black, usually with one flat color field as the background. No more than 4 colors in total, with zero texture or grain. Even shadeless lighting, with no shadows, gradients, highlights, or photographic depth.

Space: flat plane with no spatial depth, drawn close to the surface like a cartoon. Figures and symbols fill the frame in a dense, lively, rhythmic all-over composition with clear silhouette separation.

No lettering or written words. No photo, no photorealistic rendering, no camera bokeh, no film grain, no watercolor texture, no brush stroke, no airbrushed gradient.

Keep the same aspect ratio as the attached image.
```

## Observed in references

The user's original reference image isn't stored. It showed these traits, which the prompt doesn't state. Add the ones you want to the image prompt.

- **Background:** a saturated yellow field, with figures alternating red and blue.
- **Spacing:** forms barely touch, with yellow gaps between them, rather than truly overlapping.
- **Accents:** small circles, triangles, squares, and hearts fill the gaps.
