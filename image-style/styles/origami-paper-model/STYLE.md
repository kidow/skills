# Origami Paper Model

A photo rebuilt as a photographed scene of real folded-paper origami models, with crisp creases, faceted planes, and soft studio light.

## Prompt

```text
Convert the attached photo into a photograph of a handcrafted origami scene. Rebuild the subject and the main parts of its setting as real 3D models folded from paper.

Folding: every form is made of folded paper, with crisp creases, sharp ridges, and flat faceted planes like geometric origami. Simplify curves into angular folds while keeping the subject's silhouette, proportions, pose, and key details recognizable. Show small details, such as faces, hair, wheels, or leaves, as smaller folded or layered pieces.

Paper: use matte origami paper with visible fiber texture, slightly rounded fold edges, and tiny imperfections that show it was folded by hand. Match each part's paper color to the main colors in the photo, using a limited set of solid paper colors.

Scene: keep the photo's composition and arrangement. Make the setting from the same folded paper, simplified into a few larger forms such as folded walls, ground, or plants, and keep only setting elements that are in the photo.

Lighting and camera: soft, directional studio light that casts gentle shadows and shows the depth of every fold, with a shallow depth of field like a macro photograph of a real paper model. The result looks like a real photograph, not a 3D render or flat illustration.

Don't include any text, logos, or watermarks.

Keep the same aspect ratio as the attached image.
```

## Observed in references

These traits come from viewing Google image results for "origami style prompt". The images weren't stored. Add the ones you want to the image prompt. This style follows the photographed 3D model approach that dominates those results.

- **Backgrounds:** many show a single model on a plain, soft-colored background, such as warm beige, teal, or red.
- **Scenes:** landscape versions build forests, mountains, and deserts from layered folded paper, often in warm sunset light.
- **People:** portraits range from fully folded faces to realistic faces wearing folded paper clothing.
- **Monochrome:** some models are folded from plain white or cream paper only.
- **Flat versions:** a few results are flat vector or sticker illustrations of origami, which this style doesn't follow.
